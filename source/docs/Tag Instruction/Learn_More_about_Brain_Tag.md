## Learn more about Brain Tag

### Sleep Mode

Sleep tracking requires a higher standard of comfort and signal consistency as it's very important for long-term sleep tracking. Therefore, **sleep mode is only available for the Brain Tag.**

- **Unique Design for Comfort:** 

The brain tag features a customized tape to fix it around one ear. Weighing just 6g and smaller than a five-cent coin, it remains comfortable in any sleep position.

- **Stable Signal:** 

The stable fix ensures consistent and reliable signal quality throughout the night.



### **EEG is the Benchmark of Sleep Tracking.**

With EEG signals, the brain tag can measure unique brain activities at night:

- **Sleep Spindles:** Brief bursts of brain activity (11-16 Hz) during Stage 2 NREM sleep, essential for memory consolidation, learning enhancement, and stable, uninterrupted sleep.
- **K-Complex:** Distinctive waveforms during NREM sleep, crucial for deep and steady sleep, facilitating the transition from Stage 1 to Stage 2.
- **Delta Waves:** Prominent in Stages 3 and 4 of NREM sleep (slow-wave sleep), ranging from 0.5 to 4 Hz, vital for physical recovery, immune function, learning, and memory retention.



Additionally, the tag provides a comprehensive sleep report due to its multiple sensors besides EEG & PPG sensors that the headband has.

- **Temperature:** Monitors body temperature fluctuations, offering insights into your sleep cycle and sleep environment.
- **6-Axis Accelerometer:** Analyzes your movements in three-dimensional space to monitor and understand sleep positions and patterns.
