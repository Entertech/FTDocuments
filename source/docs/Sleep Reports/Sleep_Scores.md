## Sleep Score

The Flowtime Sleep Score provides a comprehensive evaluation of your sleep quality, ranging from 0 to 100. This score is categorized into four grades to help you understand your sleep performance:

- **Pay Attention:** 0-59
- **Fair:** 60-69
- **Good:** 70-84
- **Optimal:** 85-100



### How is the Score Calculated?

Your Sleep Score is calculated based on five key dimensions: Time Asleep, REM & Deep Sleep, Sleep HRV, Stability Index, and Spindle Index. Each dimension is weighted differently to reflect its impact on overall sleep quality.

| Dimensions       | Weights | Formula                                          | Notes                                                        |
| ---------------- | ------- | ------------------------------------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| Time asleep      | 35      | Total time / Goal time * 35                      | The default goal is 8 hours. You can set your own. Try to reach your goal. |
| REM & deep sleep | 20      | ((REM + Deep sleep time) / Total time / 40%)* 25 | The ideal percentage of REM  and deep sleep is 40%.          |
| Sleep HRV        | 15      | HRV index * 100% * 15                            | 0-100                                                        |
| Stability index  | 15      | Stability index * 100% * 15                      | 0-100                                                        |
| Spindle Index    | 15      | Spindle index * 100% * 15                        | 0-100, the higher the score, the better you can have a sound sleep, not disrupted by others or the environment. |
